Important Notice While in the stands or on the field with the band, alumni are considered members of the Bulldog Marching Band and are expected to follow instructions from band leadership. Alcohol is not permitted in the stands with the band.

Logistics

Parking

Closest lot is P1; P4, P30, and P31 are also nearby. P27 is reserved for University Housing residents, even on game days. No parking pass required.

Check-In

Make a name tag, pick up a polo (if pre-paid, while supplies last), and get instructions for any pre-paid rental instrument.

Rental Instrument Checkout/Check-In

Rental instruments and any included accessories (case, carrier, stand) are assigned at check-in and become your responsibility. Return to the designated volunteer in the Music Building no later than one hour after the game ends; unreturned instruments carry a fine up to their value.

Guard Equipment Checkout/Check-In

Registered color guard members are assigned a numbered flag with silk at check-in and are responsible for it until it’s returned within an hour of the game’s end; the same fine policy applies.

Music Selections

The halftime piece and a set of stands tunes are shared ahead of the event, with recordings and sheet music provided so alumni can prepare in advance.

Color Guard Choreography

Choreography is shared once it’s finalized ahead of the event.

Instrument & Case Storage

The band room is the best place to store cases during rehearsal and the game; anything brought into the stadium stays under the bleachers at your seat.

Meals

A light breakfast is provided before rehearsal.

Getting to the Stadium

Alumni can march over with the band, meet the group for tailgate performances, or arrive directly to watch pregame from the stands — each option has its own call time.

Entering the Stadium

Directions differ depending on whether you’re tailgating or heading straight to your seat.

In the Stands

Alumni play along with tunes called by the band and are asked to represent the band and university to the same standard as current members.

Halftime & Post-Halftime

Alumni perform at halftime, then can return to the stands or head to the band room, which stays open with the game on the screen.

Rental Return & Belongings

All rental instruments, guard equipment, and personal items must be collected within the return window after the game — no exceptions.
