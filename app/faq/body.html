R

Registration

Sign up & save your spot
Do I need to register for both Weekend One events?

Yes, each event (Centennial Celebration and the Halftime Show) requires separate registration so we can plan attendance accordingly.

Is there a registration fee?

Some events are free to attend, while others (like the Dave & Busters fundraiser) may have a suggested donation or ticket cost. Details are listed on each event’s registration page.

Can I register on behalf of a group?

Yes — group registration is available. Contact the alumni office if you’re coordinating for 10+ attendees.

What’s the deadline to register?

We recommend registering at least one week before each event to guarantee accommodations, though walk-ins are welcome as space allows.

S

Event Schedule

Times, places & weather
What time does the Centennial Celebration start?

Friday, September 25th from 5:30 PM–8:00 PM at the Satellite Student Union.

When is the halftime show?

Saturday, September 26th, kickoff at 7:00 PM at Fresno State, with a rehearsal beforehand.

Is there a rain/weather contingency plan?

Outdoor portions of the weekend have indoor backup locations; updates will be posted on this site and emailed to registered guests.

Will there be parking available?

Yes, designated alumni parking will be available near the Satellite Student Union and the stadium — details will be emailed closer to the event.

A

Alumni Participation

Play with the band again
Can alumni perform in the halftime show?

Yes! Alumni are invited to join current band members for the “BMB Through the Years” halftime performance — rehearsal attendance is required.

What years of alumni are welcome?

All alumni across the full 100-year history of the program are welcome to participate or attend.

Do I need my instrument?

If you’re performing, bring your own instrument if possible; a limited number of loaners may be available upon request.

Is there an alumni-only meetup during the weekend?

Yes, a reunion gathering is part of the Friday Centennial Celebration.

D

Donations & Fundraising

Support the next 100 years
How can I contribute to the centennial celebration?

Through the Dave & Busters gift card fundraiser, direct donations, or the upcoming monthly profit-share program.

Where do proceeds go?

Funds support band program scholarships, equipment, and future centennial programming.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, donations are tax-deductible; a receipt will be provided upon request.

How can my business become a sponsor?

Reach out via the contact form on this site for sponsorship packages and opportunities.
