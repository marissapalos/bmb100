BMB Through the Years Halftime Show

We are excited to welcome band alumni back to campus to celebrate 100 years of the Fresno State Bulldog Marching Band and join the current band on the field at halftime for a special halftime performance: BMB Through the Years! This opportunity is a halftime musical performance only — no drill movement. There will also be no tailgate performance and no playing in the stands. Please carefully read all of the information below.

Event Details

Parking

The closest parking lot to the music building is P1. Other nearby lots include P4, P30, and P31. As of the fall 2024 semester, P27 (dorms) is restricted to University Housing residents only, even on game days. You can view an interactive campus map here. You do not need a parking pass. However, if there is a parking attendant at the time you are entering the lot, just inform them that you are with the marching band and they should let you through. (Note: this generally only works for lots near the music building.) Please also refer to the football parking map if you plan to park somewhere other than the lots mentioned above.

Check-In

When you arrive at the Fresno State Music Building, you will be required to check in. At that time, you will be asked to make a name tag, you will receive a polo if you paid for one, and be given instructions for checking out an instrument if you paid for a rental. Additional polos will be available in a limited quantity while supplies last.

Rental Instrument Check-Out/Check-In

If you reserved and prepaid for a rental instrument, you will be assigned an instrument and serial number when you check in, at which time the instrument will be supplied to you. You will be assuming full responsibility for the instrument and any additional components included with it, including but not limited to a case, carrier, or stand. Rental instruments must be returned to the designated volunteer at the marching band truck outside the stadium immediately following the conclusion of halftime. Failure to return the instrument assigned to you will result in a monetary fine up to the value of the instrument.

Color Guard Equipment Check-Out/Check-In

Individuals registered as color guard will be assigned a numbered flag with silk when you check in. You will be assuming full responsibility for the equipment. Equipment must be returned to the designated volunteer in the music building no later than an hour after the conclusion of the football game. Failure to return the equipment assigned to you will result in a monetary fine up to the value of the equipment.

Music Selections

The halftime show has been affectionately titled BMB Through the Years, containing selections from throughout the history of the BMB that honor both Fresno State and BMB traditions. The musical selections include the original Fresno State College rendition of Fight Varsity (“the 6/8 version”), Jim’s Tune (the “Bulldog Spirit” song), The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly, and Sell Out. Due to the limited rehearsal time, we ask that you try to familiarize yourself with BMB Through the Years in advance. Music does not need to be memorized and may be used during the halftime performance. If you choose to print the music, you must do so in advance; bring your preferred viewing method, such as a phone, lyre, or flip folder.

Download sheet music and MIDI recordings below. We do not have recordings with battery percussion available. We apologize for the inconvenience.

Color Guard Choreography

Color guard choreography will be available here after 9/15. Thank you for your patience while the Bulldog Marching Band Color Guard staff prioritizes preparing the BMB Color Guard for earlier performances.

Instrument and Case Storage

The band room will be available all day for instrument and case storage, but will not be actively monitored. Please keep cases in the band room or somewhere else you feel comfortable. If you must bring a case to the stadium, you will keep it underneath the bleachers where you sit. Cases will not be allowed to be stored anywhere else at the stadium other than where you are sitting. We strongly encourage you to leave your case on campus to avoid any potential issues with security. If you require assistance transporting a larger instrument, please let us know in advance, and we will arrange for the BMB Equipment Crew to assist.

Personal instruments, rented instruments issued to you, and any applicable components are your responsibility. The Band Alumni Club, Bulldog Marching Band, and Fresno State are not responsible for lost or stolen instruments under any circumstances.

Food & Beverage

Food and beverage will not be provided at all for this event. Water jugs and cups will be provided in the stands.

Getting to Valley Children’s Stadium

The Bulldog Marching Band will be stepping off at 5pm (2 hours prior to kickoff) from the music building. If you wish to walk alongside them you may do so. However, we ask that you not play along with them for this event.

Those who had their large instruments transported to the stadium by the BMB Equipment Crew will meet at 6pm in the basin lot at the stadium complex to pick up their instruments. The truck will close at 6:15pm.

Call time for all alumni will be 6:15pm at the southeast entrance of the stadium next to the football team semi trailer, near the locker rooms.

Entering Valley Children’s Stadium

If you are not marching to the stadium with the BMB, but plan to tailgate, we will be meeting in the west end of the blue lot near the ticket booths. This is also where the BMB takes a water break after they march over. If you are not tailgating and will be meeting us in the stadium, please enter on the south side of the stadium. If entering from Bulldog Lane/Blue Lot, you will go between the red wall and the locker rooms and hang a left. If coming from Cedar/Red Lot, you will make your way between the soccer field and baseball stadium. A map is available here. (Please disregard “tailgate meetup” on the map.)

In the Stands

For this event, alumni will sit behind the BMB sousaphone section and will not participate in playing in the stands. We ask that you please save your chops and focus for halftime! As a reminder, if you do have your own tickets to the game, you may sit in those seats as long as you make the call time specified on the itinerary.

When in the stands and on the field with the Bulldog Marching Band, we ourselves are now members of the Bulldog Marching Band and must represent them and the university with dignity. Please follow instructions from the band director and other marching band leadership. There is absolutely NO ALCOHOL allowed in the stands with the BMB. If you are found drinking in the stands during the game, you will be asked to leave. Thank you for your cooperation!

Halftime Performance

Please be attentive and adaptive for instructions on how to get to and from the field at the football game. Please refer to the itinerary and instructions given day-of for tentative timing.

Post-Halftime & Rental Instrument Check-In

After halftime, alumni are welcome to return to the stands to continue watching the game or may leave for the evening. Those who rented large instruments from Fresno State will need to return them immediately to the truck. The band room will be open after halftime.
